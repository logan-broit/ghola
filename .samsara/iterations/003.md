# Iteration 3 (2026-04-11, samsara)

**What**: Switch all FTS ranking from `ts_rank` to `ts_rank_cd` (cover density ranking).

**Hypothesis**: Concept enrichment caused hundreds of sessions to tie at maximum `ts_rank`
for common query terms. `ts_rank_cd` rewards term proximity (cover density) and should break
ties. Feature binding in episodic memory (Treisman, 1996).

**Result**: R@5 29.2% -> 28.6% (-0.6pp). REGRESSION. Reverted.

**Root cause**: ts_rank_cd produces smaller absolute FTS values for most matches. While it
breaks ties in ORDER BY, the smaller values reduce FTS contribution to scoring via tanh(fts_rank).
Knowledge-update took the biggest hit (-6.4pp) because it relied on high FTS scores.

**Key insight**: The 0.6/0.4 semantic/FTS weight ratio means even perfect FTS matches can't
overcome a cosine_sim advantage of >0.33. Single-session-user requires encoding-time changes.
